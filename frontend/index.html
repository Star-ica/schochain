How it works

🏛️ Providers

Register your organization, create a program with eligibility rules, a deadline and a fixed award, then fund the pool in BOT or USDT. Funds are locked in a per-program treasury.

🧑‍🎓 Students

Connect a wallet, check the requirements, declare eligibility and apply. Track your status and claim an approved award straight to your own wallet.

🔍 Reviewers

Providers authorize reviewers per program. Reviewers see only their assigned applications and submit a score and recommendation before any decision can be made.

The award lifecycle

  1. Apply. One application per wallet. Eligibility is declared publicly; documents stay with the student, only a hash is recorded.
  2. Review. Assigned reviewers score the application. A provider-set quorum must be reached first.
  3. Decide & reserve. Approving reserves the award from the pool immediately, so promises can never exceed funded money or the award cap.
  4. Publish. Decisions become official when the provider publishes them.
  5. Claim. The student claims once, within the claim window. Unclaimed awards expire and funds return to the pool; surplus is refundable after the program closes.
What is — and isn't — privateBlockchains are public. SchoChain never asks for names, IDs, transcripts or financial records onchain. A document hash lets you prove a file existed unchanged; it does not make the file private, and it is not storage. Keep your documents with the provider through a channel you trust.
